## Honest read (locked metrics)

- **Nipun** — clone WINS (point estimate) on: fire recall on visible ticks 79% (majority 0%).
- **Nipun** — clone LOSES (point estimate) on: cross-clone's balanced move (15%) >= clone (14%); a baseline had smaller moved-position error (clone 3.42, best baseline 3.16).
  - **Significance (non-overlapping 95% CIs, 5 rounds):** balanced move vs majority separates, vs cross-clone OVERLAPS; fire recall vs majority separates. Treat overlapping differences as directional, not proven. The intervals will tighten once a player has 3+ matches.
  - Why the clone and bot converge on move/position: 1–2 training matches means the nearest neighbor is often far, so the breaker (fired 5% of ticks) hands to the basic bot — itself a baseline. Data limit, not a metric trick.
- **What-if:** best-plan win vs the clone median 100% vs the basic bot median 60%. The clone now fires 22% of visible ticks vs the bot's 84%. The clone still fires less than the bot, so treat clone win rates as a ceiling until more firing data arrives.
